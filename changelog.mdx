## Manage Your Workspace from Chat

**Workspace actions in chat:** Ask the Research Assistant to create or update projects, rename and move records, archive or restore them, reset failed processing, update record setup, and preview or run report generation — without leaving the conversation.

**Understands names, not IDs:** Refer to projects and records by name, exactly as you would in conversation. The assistant searches your workspace, finds the right item, and completes the action — including multi-step requests like "show the records in this project, then rename it".

**Confirmation for sensitive actions:** Archiving, resetting, and paid report generation show a confirmation card with the expected token cost before anything runs. Generation uses the same Aseed token balance and billing rules as the app.

**Live updates:** Completed actions appear as cards in the chat, and your project and record lists refresh automatically.

**Responds in your language:** The assistant answers workspace requests in the language you write in.

Learn more: [Research Assistant](/projects/context)

</Update>

## Manage Aseed from Your AI Client

**Projects:** Create projects and update their name, description, status, research goals, and interview guide from Cursor, Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, or another compatible MCP client.

**Records:** Rename records, move them between projects, archive or restore them, safely reset failed processing, and update goals, guides, meeting notes, custom prompts, participant details, languages, and speech-to-text settings.

**Report generation:** List available prompts and presets, preview the exact expected token cost, generate or regenerate single-record reports, create project-report configurations, and run project reports in Fast or Thinking mode. Generation uses the same Aseed token balance and billing rules as the app.

**Safe write access:** New connections receive the supported read and write permissions by default. Reversible or paid actions require explicit confirmation, ownership is checked on every operation, and idempotent retries prevent duplicate objects, jobs, and token charges.

<Note>
  If you connected Aseed MCP before version 1.5.1, disconnect and reconnect it to review and approve the new write permissions. Existing read-only grants are not expanded automatically.
</Note>

File upload, deletion, transcript editing, token purchase, public-sharing controls, and AI Interviewer room management are not included in this release.

Learn more: [Aseed MCP](/sharing/mcp)

</Update>

## Aseed MCP Server

**Connect your AI client:** Link Cursor, Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, or another MCP client to Aseed at `https://app.aseed.ai/mcp`.

**Read your workspace:** Search and fetch projects, records, transcripts, reports, AI Interviewer room metadata, and your token balance. Access follows your Aseed account permissions.

**Read-only:** The server does not upload, edit, delete, generate reports, start interviews, or spend tokens. You sign in with browser OAuth.

Learn more: [Aseed MCP](https://docs.aseed.ai/sharing/mcp)

</Update>

## Research Assistant: context briefs

Generate research context briefs through an interactive Q&A session in the Research Assistant.

**Features:**

- **Interactive Q&A**: Answer questions about your research project to generate a structured 11-section brief covering metadata, decision framing, product context, goals, hypotheses, participants, method, and analysis

- **Two Input Methods**: Answer questions step by step or provide existing research materials upfront

- **Save to Projects**: Save generated briefs directly to your projects, download as Markdown, or copy to clipboard

- **Fast & Thinking Modes**: Choose between Fast (2 tokens) or Thinking (5 tokens) modes

- **Additional Options**: Skip questions, modify existing briefs, and get validation with follow-up questions
